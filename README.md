## 🧪 Breaking Bad

**Created by:** Vince Gilligan
**Network:** AMC
**Seasons:** 5 (62 episodes)

A mild-mannered high school chemistry teacher is diagnosed with cancer and turns to manufacturing drugs to secure his family's future. What starts as a desperate choice becomes a slow, gripping transformation.

### Why I love it

- 🎭 Incredible character development
- 🎬 Tight, deliberate storytelling
- 🧠 Every decision has consequences
- 📸 Gorgeous cinematography

### Main Characters

1. **Walter White**: the teacher turned kingpin
2. **Jesse Pinkman**: his former student and partner
3. **Skyler White**: Walter's wife
4. **Hank Schrader**: Walter's brother-in-law and a DEA agent

> *"I am the one who knocks."*
> Walter White

## ⚖️ Better Call Saul

**Prequel to:** Breaking Bad
**Seasons:** 6 (63 episodes)

The story of Jimmy McGill, a small-time lawyer, and how he becomes the flashy criminal attorney Saul Goodman. It's a slow-burn character study that rivals its parent show.

<details>
<summary>🔍 Click to expand: Why it might be even better than Breaking Bad</summary>

- Deeper character study of Jimmy and Kim
- Brilliant use of the **slow burn**
- Chuck vs. Jimmy is one of TV's best sibling conflicts
- Stunning visual storytelling

</details>
